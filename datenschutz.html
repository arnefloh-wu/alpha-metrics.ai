Website storage and optional measurement

By default the homepage does not load advertising measurement tools. Your theme and advertising measurement choices are stored locally in your browser. The advertising measurement choice is remembered for up to 180 days. The contact form works regardless of your advertising measurement choice.

Optional ChatGPT advertising measurement

If you affirmatively allow advertising measurement on the homepage, we load the OpenAI Measurement Pixel to assess the effectiveness of ChatGPT advertisements. We request page-view and successful-contact-submission events; the latter is not triggered by merely opening or attempting to submit the contact form. The integration does not explicitly pass the form fields to the event API. The SDK may process browser and device information, IP address, page URL, advertising identifiers and cookies. For Austrian Web Pixels, automatic advanced matching is enabled: the SDK can recognise supported contact information on the website, hash it in the browser using SHA-256 and include the hashes in measurement events. Hashing is not anonymisation. We do not intentionally send the contents of your enquiry to OpenAI.

The legal basis for optional measurement is consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and applicable rules on terminal-device access). Declining does not prevent use of the website. To withdraw or change your consent, open Advertising privacy settings on the homepage and select Decline. Withdrawal does not affect processing that took place before withdrawal.

The Pixel uses the first-party cookies __oppref (advertising click reference; 30 days after writing) and __obref (browser reference; 365 days after creation). Browser settings may shorten those periods. The Pixel removes these cookies when measurement consent is withdrawn. These cookie lifetimes are not the retention periods for conversion data.

Recipient for EEA data: OpenAI Ireland Ltd., Dublin, Ireland. Under the Ad Tools Data Processing Addendum, the parties generally act as independent controllers; for restricted processing, OpenAI acts as processor. The Addendum requires a valid transfer mechanism for onward transfers of EEA data to jurisdictions without adequate protection. See the Conversion Terms and OpenAI privacy policy. Confirm the applicable account terms, conversion-data retention criteria, and the appropriate disclosure of transfer safeguards before activation.

Contact form

If you send a message, the details you enter (name, email address, company, message) are transmitted to our form provider Formspree (Formspree, Inc., USA), which forwards them to us by email. We use them only to answer your enquiry. The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) and, where a contract may follow, pre-contractual measures (Art. 6(1)(b)). You can withdraw consent at any time. We delete the data once the enquiry is settled, unless the law requires us to keep it. Confirm the data processing agreement with Formspree.
