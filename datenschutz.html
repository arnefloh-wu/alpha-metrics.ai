2. Hosting and DNS

Our website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). Access to the site involves technical request information, including your IP address, which the hosting provider may process in logs to operate, protect and troubleshoot its service. Our legal basis is our legitimate interest in reliable and secure website operation (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Cloudflare, Inc. (USA) provides our domain and DNS services. The website's DNS records are configured as DNS only: HTTP requests go to GitHub Pages rather than through Cloudflare's reverse proxy. DNS requests may involve technical identifiers and request information. Our legal basis is our legitimate interest in making the website accessible and secure (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Cloudflare's Self-Serve Subscription Agreement incorporates its Data Processing Addendum for covered data. Further information is available in Cloudflare's privacy policy.

3. Local storage and your choices

Your browser stores your chosen light or dark theme locally to preserve your requested display preference. Your advertising measurement preference is also stored locally so that your choice can be respected, for up to 180 days before a new decision is required. These preferences are not transmitted by our preference-storage code. You can clear them in your browser settings. The legal basis for remembering these choices is our legitimate interest in providing the requested functionality and respecting your privacy preferences (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

4. Optional ChatGPT advertising measurement

Only if you actively choose Allow measurement, we load the OpenAI Measurement Pixel to evaluate the performance of our ChatGPT ads. It records website visits and successfully submitted contact enquiries, not unsuccessful attempts. OpenAI may use conversion data to provide campaign reports and to measure, deliver and improve advertising services under its conversion terms. Our event configuration opts out of future user-level personalisation.

The information processed may include browser and device data, IP address, website origin, event type and timestamp, advertising and browser identifiers and cookies. Automatic advanced matching can recognise supported customer identifiers, such as email addresses, and transmit SHA-256 hashes of those identifiers. Hashing does not make personal data anonymous. Our event integration does not explicitly supply your contact form fields or enquiry text to OpenAI.

With your consent, the Pixel may set two first-party cookies:

  • __oppref: advertising click reference, expiring 30 days after the Pixel last writes it.
  • __obref: randomly generated browser reference, expiring 365 days after creation.

Browser restrictions may shorten these periods. The Pixel's consent control removes these cookies upon withdrawal. Cookie expiry is not the same as the period for which conversion data is retained on OpenAI's systems.

The legal basis for this optional processing is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and applicable Austrian rules on storing and accessing information on terminal devices). Refusing consent does not restrict access to our website or contact form. To change or withdraw your choice, use Advertising privacy settings on the homepage and select Decline. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.

For EEA data the recipient is OpenAI Ireland Limited, Dublin, Ireland. Under its Ad Tools Data Processing Addendum, OpenAI generally processes data as an independent controller; for restricted processing it acts as processor. The addendum requires lawful safeguards for onward transfers. OpenAI identifies standard contractual clauses for transfers between its affiliates in its Ad Tools Sub-Processor List. The Conversion Terms govern the use of conversion data.

OpenAI's published general retention criteria include the need to provide its services, resolve disputes, maintain security and comply with legal obligations. The public Pixel documentation does not specify a fixed server-side conversion-data retention period. For details on retention or applicable transfer safeguards, contact privacy@openai.com. See also OpenAI's European privacy policy and privacy request portal.

(USA), which processes the submission and forwards it by email. We use your information to respond to your enquiry and, where appropriate, discuss a potential project, training engagement or contract. The legal basis is your consent to the form transmission (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), pre-contractual steps at your request where applicable (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR), or our legitimate interest in responding to business correspondence (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

The contact and quote forms require your name, email address, message and consent to the transmission. Quote forms automatically identify the service you selected. The company field is optional. Without the required information we cannot process a form submission; you may instead contact us by email. Advertising measurement consent is not required. Please do not include sensitive personal data or confidential research datasets in an initial enquiry.

Formspree states that it uses standard contractual clauses for its processing services. Its security information and privacy policy describe its practices. Submissions may remain stored by Formspree after they are no longer visible in the account's submission history.

Incoming email to our business domain is forwarded through Cloudflare Email Routing to a consumer Gmail mailbox (Google). These services process sender and recipient details, message content and delivery information. Google's consumer service is subject to its privacy policy; Google does not provide a processor DPA for consumer Gmail. Our legitimate interest is receiving, managing and responding to business correspondence, or the processing is necessary for requested pre-contractual steps where applicable.

We retain enquiries and correspondence for as long as necessary to deal with your request, manage any resulting business relationship, comply with statutory retention duties or establish, exercise or defend legal claims. You may request deletion of data that is no longer necessary. Storage by service providers, including backups, is subject to their applicable retention arrangements.
