# alpha-metrics.ai brand voice

Read by every content agent and by `claim-checker`. Derived from the live site.

## Voice in four words
Precise. Calm. Sceptical. Useful.

## Rules
1. **Every number carries its base**, and its interval where one exists:
   "38% (95% KI 34-42%, n=512)". If the base is unknown, do not use the number.
2. **No claim without a source.** Own data: name the study, n and field period. Other
   data: link the primary source. No "studies show".
3. **Association is not effect.** Write "hängt zusammen mit / is associated with" unless
   the design is experimental or longitudinal and supports a causal word.
4. **Label synthetic data, every time.** ZINTO is "eine fiktive Marke mit synthetischen
   Daten / a fictional brand with synthetic data".
5. **No hype words**: revolutionary, game-changer, disruptive, cutting-edge, unlock,
   leverage, "in today's fast-paced world", rocket and fire emoji.
6. **No unprovable superlatives** (UWG): not "best", "leading", "most accurate".
   Allowed: concrete, checkable statements ("every estimate with its 95% interval").
7. **Talk about the reader's decision**, not our process, in the first two lines.
8. **One idea per post / section.**
9. **Short sentences, active voice, no jargon without a one-line explanation.**
10. **Honest about limits**: say what the data cannot show; it is our signature.

## German
- Formal "Sie" in ads, site and newsletter; LinkedIn posts by the founder may use a
  neutral impersonal style and avoid both "du" and "Sie" where possible.
- Austrian usage where natural (Jänner, heuer is fine in AT posts; avoid in DE ads).
- Terms: Konfidenzintervall (KI), Fallzahl / Basis, Gewichtung, Designeffekt,
  gestützte / ungestützte Bekanntheit, Markenfunnel, Treiberanalyse.

## English
- British spelling, as on the site (analyse, colour, programme).

## LinkedIn specifics
- Hook in line 1 (max ~12 words), a concrete tension, not a question for engagement.
- 120-220 words. Line breaks between ideas. At most 3 hashtags, at the end.
- Link in the first comment, not the post body, unless it is a Document/Article.
- End with a sentence that teaches, not "What do you think? 👇".

## Do / don't
| Don't | Do |
|---|---|
| "Brand awareness skyrocketed by 5%!" | "Awareness rose 5 points; after removing the method effect, 2 points remain (95% interval 0.4-3.6)." |
| "Social media drives purchase." | "Buyers mention social media more often than non-buyers. That is an association; the order of contacts was not recorded." |
| "Germany shows the same trend." | "In Germany only 41 respondents knew the brand, too few to report the funnel." |
