About

Research-led, built for decisions

alpha-metrics.ai is an independent research and AI practice based in Vienna. We bring scientific thinking and practical methods to marketing, business decision-making and AI adoption.

Our four service areas complement one another: understand customers and markets, test advertising, identify useful applications of AI, and equip your team to work with it. We agree clear objectives and deliverables with you, and communicate limitations openly.
