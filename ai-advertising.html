02 / AI Advertising

Advertise in the age of conversational AI.

Plan, run and evaluate advertising on AI platforms such as ChatGPT, where available. Combine campaign management with experimental research to learn which messages perform and where your media budget is best used.

Experimental design

Define hypotheses, competing creative variants, the primary outcome, budget and stopping rule before launch. Change one element at a time where feasible.

Campaign setup and management

Configure client-owned campaigns, keep targeting and settings comparable, and monitor delivery. Platform and market eligibility are checked before launch.

Advertising effectiveness

Analyse impressions, clicks, click-through rates and costs from aggregate reports. Quantify differences, uncertainty and delivery imbalances; assess conversions where tracking permits.

Organic AI brand visibility

Separately audit brand mentions and recommendations across a defined set of prompts, recording the model, market and date. Paid placement is not a proxy for organic visibility.

A small pilot, a clear decision

Compare two value propositions — for example, taste versus a functional benefit — with a fixed media budget and a predefined reporting period. Receive a management summary, a documented research protocol and reproducible analysis.

Performance is not necessarily causality. Without verified random allocation, a comparison is a quasi-experimental field study. Algorithmic delivery, repeated impressions and aggregate reporting limit causal inference and audience-level conclusions. We report those limits rather than treating a higher click-through rate as proof of a causal effect.

  • Decide the goal first. Campaign goal and conversion event cannot be changed once a campaign exists.
  • Measure what the campaign counts. Our pixel did not send the event the campaign optimised for until we changed the site code.
  • Plan budgets on real prices. In our first days, 1,000 impressions cost about €190. At that price a few hundred euros buy only a small sample for an A/B test.
